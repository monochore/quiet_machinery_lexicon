QUIET MACHINERY

I. ASPERITAS

"A sound, once struck, belongs to the elements. Asperitas is the weather."
A granular delay and looping environment designed to capture complex transients and subject them to gradual, organic decay. It catches a live acoustic artifact in amber and immediately surrenders it to erosion. High frequencies rust. Structural attacks blur into heavy, breathing phase swells. There is no preservation here—only the slow, alchemical decay of a monument returning to the earth, dragged through a suffocating thicket and eaten by its own echoes.
SOURCE (Ingestion & Capture)
Sublimate Strips hard transients with 1.2-second volume swells, softening the incoming attack.
Amber I & Amber II Provide 2-second and 4-second click-free capture buffers to trap moments in time.
Undertow Crossfades the playback between forward and reverse, disorienting the temporal flow.
WEATHERING (Degradation & Friction)
Patina Dictates high-frequency signal loss and introduces heavy analog distortion (pushed up to 9.0x drive), causing frozen audio to progressively darken and saturate with each repetition.
Thicket Acts as an oppressive low-pass filter, dragging the ceiling from 18kHz down to a suffocating 400Hz.
Tide Splits the live signal to simulate massive, breathing phase swells that move across the stereo field.
THE DEEP (Space & Time)
Alembic Controls a hard-capped infinite reverb tank, washing the signal into an impenetrable fog.
Transmute Drags three cascading delay read-heads entirely off the rigid grid, forcing them into sprawling polyrhythms spanning up to 80,000 samples.
Sway Introduces seasick, tape-like wow and flutter.
Distill Brick-walls the resulting chaotic echoes into a compressed, polished pad.
MACROS (Autonomous Sabotage)
Ouroboros Routes 40% of the spatial effect back into the weathering system, forcing the machine to endlessly eat its own tail.
Entropy Utilizes a bipolar random voltage to actively modulate Thicket, Transmute, and Sway without user input, ensuring the environment remains unpredictable and organically shifting.

II. KARST

"Rhythm is born from friction. Karst forces four asynchronous plates to grind against one another in the dark."
A rhythmic synthesis environment converting geological pressure into physical structures, built to actively resist and defamiliarize the grid. There is no master clock, only mass, matter, and resistance. Microscopic faults give way to skeletal ticks, and weight accumulates in the mantle until the pressure forces a heavy, metallic strike over a lightless void. It is the slow, violent ritual of mountain-building, where time is measured exclusively in pressure and collapse.
MOTORS (Asynchronous Drive)
Strata Scales the base frequency of the rhythmic generation.
Shear Introduces microscopic, off-grid timing offsets that create organic push and pull.
Erosion Utilizes a cubic-curve probability gate to violently dissolve the generated rhythms into quiet, skeletal, and unpredictable ticks.
MATERIAL (Physicality & Tone)
Mass (Per Voice: Mantle, Shadow, Flint, Apex) Controls the fundamental weight and sub-frequency using sharp exponential curves.
Matter (Per Voice: Mantle, Shadow, Flint, Apex) Dictates the physical decay time and introduces sharp metallic resonance.
Slip (Per Voice: Mantle, Shadow, Flint, Apex) Allows for manual, unstepped offset phase-shifting.
Fluid (Per Voice: Mantle, Shadow, Flint, Apex) Overrides the percussive triggers entirely, forcing sharp strikes into unending, sustained drone states.
FX/VOID (Atmosphere & Absence)
Bake Applies a non-linear analog warmth and hard limiting to the sum of the voices, gluing the tectonic strikes together.
Ping Creates a stereo resonant reservoir that warbles and reacts directly in tandem with the Shear parameter.
Void Swallows the entire structural output into a massive, lightless reverberation chamber, washing out the rigid percussive strikes into a distant, tectonic rumble.

III. KAMEN

"The sanctuary of the grid, desecrated by physical mass."
An autonomous physical modelling ecosystem designed to replace the sterile safety of the sequencer with the brutal, shifting reality of the acoustic stopwatch and shattered stone. It does not speak MIDI, and it refuses the sterile safety of the sequencer. It understands only gravity, friction, and structural ruin. Two distinct lithic waveguides ring and grind against one another, struck by stochastic cascades of falling dust. It forces the composer into a difficult duet with a living material, replacing the digital clock with the unpredictable, heavy momentum of shattered stone.
GLOBAL (Atmosphere & Force)
Altitude Dictates the global spectral tilt by governing the tuning interval of a second, independent physical waveguide, sweeping from a sub-octave foundation up through dissonant overtones.
Incense Introduces physical friction, quietly hissing at rest but violently sparking and shattering the exact millisecond the stone is struck.
Velocity Governs the raw sensitivity and final driving force of the machine.
I. INVOCATION (Weight & Momentum)
Liturgy Acts as the fundamental vocal cord, sweeping the main physical resonator from deep, subterranean rumbles up to piercing frequencies with the precision of an analog oscillator.
Gravity Acts as a dynamic low-pass gate and envelope morph, dictating the physical mass of the strike and the damping of the stone—from massive, ringing boulders to dry percussive clicks and continuous bowed friction.
Offer Replaces the metronome with a physics-based bouncing ball generator, dropping unpredictable, rolling clusters of strikes that mathematically accelerate into skittering flutters before resetting.
II. THE MATERIAL (Stiffness & Form)

Sweeping the dial seamlessly stretches the pure, glass-like harmonics of a pristine string into the dense, stiff, clanging inharmonicity of a massive iron bell, applying deep geometric saturation and high-frequency absorption.
III. SANCTUARY (The Amber & The Ruin)

IV. MNEMOSYNE

"Memory is never retrieved whole; it is a ruin reconstructed from shattered pieces."
A live-capture rhythmic micro-slicer and mosaic environment, designed to cast suspended audio in mortar, fracture it into composed, rigid sequences, and bleed them into suffocating decay. Mnemosyne tears a single breath of audio from the room and casts it in mortar. The architecture is immediately subjected to fault lines, temporal decay, and violent dropouts. What remains is a destructive mosaic—asynchronous gates forcing the frozen trace into complex, interlocking structures. Ghosts wake up in the undercurrent. It is the act of building a heavy, ancient temple from the broken glass of the immediate past.
TRACE (Capture & Sabotage)
Trace Toggle The performance catalyst; it instantly freezes a 2-second block of live audio to act as the structural raw material.
Fault Actively sabotages the grid, either pulling the sequence into polyrhythmic phase-drift or violently fracturing it with Euclidean dropouts and mathematical silence.
SUBSTRATE (Time & Weathering)
Cadence Controls the master clock, scaled musically so that dead-center retains the exact 1:1 original pitch, while sweeping the dial pushes the architecture into deep sub-octaves or crystalline highs.
Index Unbolts the read-heads, allowing manual, physical traversal to scrub across the frozen timeline.
Erosion Shortens the transient decay tails, mutating sustained blocks into tight, percussive plucks.
Silica Subjects the shattered audio to extreme data-loss, sample-rate decimation, and razor-sharp zero-crossing errors.
TESSELLATION (Mosaic)
Block, Tile, Fragment, & Frit Block (1/4 note), Tile (1/8 note), Fragment (1/16 note), and Frit (1/32 note) govern hard-cut subdivisions. Because they read from the exact same frozen trace, they generate complex, interlocking IDM polyrhythms from a static source without pitch dissonance, building a new physical structure from the ruins.
UNDERCURRENT (Chaos & Ghosts)
